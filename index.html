Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pondi Technologies ("we", "us"), a business registered with BRELA in Tanzania (Registration No. 723134), collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit ponditech.com, contact us, or use our products, including itaOS. We aim to handle personal data in line with Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, business name, phone number, email address and message when you fill in our contact form or write to us.
  • Account information for itaOS users, such as name, email, business details and login information.
  • Business data you connect to itaOS, such as Meta ad account performance data (campaigns, spend, results) and WhatsApp Business messages, contact names and phone numbers of your customers, and payment-related messages.
  • Basic technical data, such as browser type and pages visited, collected by our hosting provider for security and performance.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, run and improve itaOS, including labelling chats with our AI assistant Kito and matching sales to ads.
  • To reply to your enquiries and provide support.
  • To keep our services secure and prevent misuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your customers' data for our own advertising.

3. Our role for customer data

When a business uses itaOS, that business decides which customer data is processed. In that case we act on the business's behalf (as a data processor) and only process that data to provide the service to them.

4. Data from Meta and WhatsApp

If you connect a Meta ad account or WhatsApp Business number, we access only the data needed to run itaOS, through the official Meta and WhatsApp Business Platform APIs. We use this data in accordance with Meta's Platform Terms and do not transfer it to data brokers or ad networks.

5. Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help us run our services, such as cloud hosting and database providers, WhatsApp Business Solution Providers and AI processing providers. They may only use the data to provide services to us. We may also disclose data where required by law.

6. International transfers

Some of our service providers store or process data outside Tanzania. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure the data stays protected.

7. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above. When a business stops using itaOS, we delete or anonymise its data within a reasonable period unless the law requires us to keep it.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect data, including encrypted connections and restricted access. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. To make a request, email hello@ponditech.com.

10. Data deletion

To delete your data, including data received through Meta or WhatsApp, email hello@ponditech.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the phone number or account the request relates to. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
